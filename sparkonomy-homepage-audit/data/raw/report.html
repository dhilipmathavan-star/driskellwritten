03Homepage overview

Sparkonomy's homepage is a single, dark, scroll-driven story ("Sparking Creator livelihoods") that introduces the company through the founders' YouTube/Google background and ends in its first product, Open Creator Graph — a free "Creator Record" that lets Creators be visible to AI and control how AI uses their identity. Every conversion point goes to the external app https://creatorgraph.sparkonomy.com.

Structure
  • A fixed, transparent header (wordmark, 4 links, gradient pill CTA; hamburger + full-screen menu below 768px) over a fixed full-viewport WebGL fluid-ink canvas and a radial vignette that stay behind everything.
  • Hero (not pinned): animated gradient "Sparkonomy" wordmark, tagline, a "NEW" launch card for Open Creator Graph with the CTA, a partner-logo marquee (Meta, YouTube, Google for Startups, Gemini) and a "READ OUR STORY" cue.
  • Six pinned story screens (position: sticky panels inside 200–420svh tall sections) whose lines reveal "beat by beat" as you scroll, with word-by-word colour "ink" effects.
  • Creator Record demo (normal flow): example record card for "Chulbuli Dixit" with per-facet AI-usage stances, plus the second CTA.
  • Closing pinned screen ("And we're only getting started." / "Let's spark together.") and a black footer.
Character

Black canvas, white Roboto type at large sizes (64px headlines at 1440px), a two-colour brand gradient (magenta #DE0075 → violet #8134AF) used for CTAs and word highlights, and constant ambient motion (fluid simulation, wordmark pulse, CTA gradient sweep). The page also auto-plays: after 10 seconds without input it starts scrolling itself through the story.

Facts
  • Document height at 1440×900: 17,082px (fully rendered; the first automated capture measured 17,374px before the lazy card settled). 390×844: 15,984px. 834×1112: 20,766px.
  • Headings: 1 H1 ("Sparkonomy"), 9 H2s, 1 H3. Links: 8 internal destinations (/, /creator/earn, /about, /contact, /blogs, /legal/privacy-policy, /legal/terms, /legal/refund-policy), 1 external app (https://creatorgraph.sparkonomy.com) linked 5 times, 5 social profiles (footer only).
  • Built with Next.js 16.0.7 (App Router, Turbopack), React 19, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion; hosted behind Google Frontend; GTM + GA4 + Microsoft Clarity + CookieYes.
